WDD 231 · Final Project Website Planning Document

Trout Restaurant at Laguna Corani

Prepared by Roberto Uriona

Overview

Purpose

The website introduces a restaurant beside Laguna Corani in Cochabamba, Bolivia, known for dishes made with locally farmed trout. It combines the restaurant's menu and visitor information with the natural setting of the lake, helping guests decide what to eat and plan how to get there.

Target Market

The primary audience is local residents, families, and travelers visiting Laguna Corani who want to enjoy a meal with a lake view. Visitors need clear menu choices and estimated prices, directions from National Route 4, business hours, and an easy way to contact the restaurant before making the trip.

Site Goals

  • Showcase the restaurant's trout dishes and the scenery around Laguna Corani.
  • Help visitors compare menu options and estimated prices before arriving.
  • Make trip planning easier with directions, hours, parking details, and contact options.
  • Encourage inquiries, reservations, and orders through a WhatsApp contact link.

User Personas

  • Weekend Traveler: Andrea is a 29-year-old visitor from Cochabamba planning a day trip to Laguna Corani. She checks directions and opening hours on her phone and wants to know whether she can stop for lunch along the way.
  • Local Family: Carlos is a 42-year-old resident organizing a meal for his family. He wants to see trout and children's meal options, estimate the cost, and ask about seating before leaving home.

Scenarios

  • A visitor wants to compare trout preparations and prices before choosing a place to eat near the lake.
  • A family member checks whether the menu includes a meal suitable for children and hot drinks.
  • A driver wants directions from National Route 4, parking information, and the restaurant's current hours.
  • A guest wants to message the restaurant on WhatsApp to ask a question or make a reservation.

SEO Plan

  • Use descriptive page titles, headings, and natural search phrases such as “trout restaurant Laguna Corani,” “where to eat at Laguna Corani,” “trucha Cochabamba,” and “restaurant near National Route 4.”
  • Write concise page descriptions and useful image alternative text that identify the restaurant, its trout dishes, and the Laguna Corani setting.
  • Publish accurate location and business details after confirming them with the restaurant. If eligible, create or update a Google Business Profile with matching hours, contact details, and map pin.
  • Keep the menu, hours, directions, and contact information current so search visitors can confidently plan a visit.

Content

Home

Welcomes visitors with the restaurant name, a short introduction to its locally farmed trout, and photographs of Laguna Corani and signature dishes. A concise call to action guides visitors to the menu or to directions.

  • Primary actions: Explore the menu and get directions.
  • Supporting content: A brief restaurant introduction and a preview of featured trout dishes.
  • Visuals: Landscape and food photography that represents the restaurant and its lakeside setting.

Menu

Presents locally farmed trout prepared with butter, garlic, or as chicharrón (fried trout), along with children's meals and hot beverages. Each item includes a brief description and an estimated price so visitors can plan their meal.

  • Menu categories: Trout dishes, children's meals, and hot beverages.
  • Prices are estimates until confirmed with the restaurant.
  • Food photographs support the descriptions without obscuring prices or item names.

Location & Contact

Helps guests reach the restaurant with a map and directions from National Route 4. The page lists business hours, parking availability, and contact details, with a WhatsApp button for inquiries, reservations, or orders.

  • Location and directions: Map and step-by-step guidance from National Route 4.
  • Visitor details: Confirmed address, operating hours, and parking information.
  • Contact: WhatsApp link with a clear label describing the action.

The exact location, operating hours, parking availability, menu descriptions, and prices must be confirmed with the restaurant before publication. Do not publish placeholder values as confirmed facts.
